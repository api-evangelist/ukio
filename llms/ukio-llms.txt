# Ukio

> Ukio is a Barcelona-founded proptech company offering monthly furnished apartment rentals across Europe — Barcelona, Madrid, Berlin, Lisbon, Milan, and Paris — plus corporate housing for companies and a property-management program for real-estate owners. Ukio publishes no public developer API, SDKs, or API documentation.
